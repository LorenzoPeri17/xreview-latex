If removing or adding a whole \texttt{align} environment is desired, \textit{xreview} offers a the \writeenv{addalign} and \writeenv{remalign} environments (as well as \writeenv{addalign*} and \writeenv{remalign*}).

\begin{center}
    \begin{minipage}[t]{0.3\linewidth}
    \textbf{\LaTeX Code}
    
    New equations:\newline
    \writecommand{begin}\writearg{addalign}

    ~\quad \& a+b = d\textbackslash\textbackslash

    ~\quad \& b+c=3d\newline
    \writecommand{end}\writearg{addalign}\newline
    Removed equations:\newline
    \writecommand{begin}\writearg{remalign}

    ~\quad \& a+b = d\textbackslash\textbackslash

    ~\quad \& b+c=3d\newline
    \writecommand{end}\writearg{remalign}
    
    \end{minipage}
    \hfill
    \begin{minipage}[t]{0.3\linewidth}
    \writecommand{showchanges}
    \showchanges
    
    New equations:
    \begin{addalign}
        & a+b = d \\
        & b+c=3d
    \end{addalign}\addtocounter{equation}{-2}
    Removed equations:
    \begin{remalign}
        & a+b = d \\
        & b+c=3d
    \end{remalign}
    \makeatletter
    \addtocounter{xreview@removedeqcounter}{-2}
    \makeatother

    \end{minipage}
    \hfill
    \begin{minipage}[t]{0.3\linewidth}

    \showclean
    
    New equations:
    \begin{addalign}
        & a+b = d \\
        & b+c=3d

    \begin{remalign}

        & b+c=3d
    \end{remalign}

\end{minipage}

While \writeenv{addalign} and \writeenv{addalign*} works almost indistinguishably from marking each line as a \writecommand{addedeqline}, this is quite clearly not the same for \writeenv{remalign}. The reason is simple: \textit{amsmath} does \textit{not} provide an easy way to \writecommand{sout} through alignment tabs and newlines. Therefore, we pull a simple trick to ``\textit{sout}'' diagonally across. Not \textit{as} pretty, but it is a drop-in replacement for a to-be-removed \writeenv{align}! If an author cares very much about every single line being \textit{beautifully} stricken-through, they unfortunately must use \writecommand{removedeqline} and friends explicitly. Because of these limitations, \writeenv{remalign} and \writeenv{remalign*} try to be as customizable as possible. Please refer to the full documentation in \texttt{xreview.pdf} for more details.
